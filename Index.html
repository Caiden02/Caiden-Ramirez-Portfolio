About Me

I am a 20-year-old second-year Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies (BAMS) student with a passion for art, creativity, and visual storytelling. I create commissioned artworks across various mediums, including paintings, sketches, and other forms of visual art. Alongside my creative pursuits, I work part-time as a barista at a local coffee shop while serving as Co-Chair of the Regen Asia Summit 2027, organized by the National University of Singapore, where I lead the planning and execution of one of Asia’s largest student-led summits. Through my experiences in art, service, and leadership, I continue to explore how creativity and collaboration can bring ideas to life and create meaningful impact.
